# Basics
§fWayfarer's Map§r draws everything you have §aalready seen§r. New places appear on the map as you explore.

- §eM§r — open / close the world map
- §eN§r — show / hide the minimap
- §e=§r and §e-§r — minimap zoom
- §eB§r — new waypoint where you stand
- §eU§r — waypoint list
- §eK§r — cave mode (auto / off / on)

> All keys can be changed in Minecraft's §bOptions → Controls§r.

# Map navigation
- §eLeft drag§r — move the map
- §eWheel§r — zoom (towards the cursor)
- §eSpace§r — back to the player
- §eRight click§r on empty space — menu: §bTeleport here§r, §bNew waypoint§r
- §eRight click§r on a waypoint — opens its editor

The bottom left shows the coordinates under the cursor. §7(?)§r means the place is not explored yet.

# Waypoints
A waypoint is a point with a §fname§r, an §foutline color§r and an §fitem icon§r. It shows on the map, the minimap and in the world.

- Create: §eB§r, §eRight click → New waypoint§r or the button in the list
- Edit: §eright click§r it on the map or in the list §b(§eU§b)
- Pick the color in the color picker: color swatch → hue, saturation, brightness
- The icon can be any item
- The map shows the §fdistance§r next to the name

> Long names are cut so labels don't overlap. Hover a waypoint to see the full name at the bottom.

# Waypoint groups
Groups hide waypoints in bulk, for example §f"Ores"§r or §f"Bases"§r.

- Create a group in the waypoint list §b(§eU§b)
- §eDrag a waypoint with the left button§r onto a group to move it
- The toggle next to a group hides / shows §fall its waypoints at once§r

# Teleport
! Teleporting works §fonly with permission for /tp§r on the server (or cheats in single player). Without it there are no teleport buttons.

- §eRight click → Teleport here§r — to the highest safe spot
- If the height is unknown (unexplored), the map §fasks for Y§r in a window
- The waypoint list has a teleport button for each waypoint

# Caves
Cave mode shows §fa layer underground§r instead of the surface.

- §bCaves§r / §eK§r: §fauto§r — on while you are underground; §fon§r / §foff§r — always
- The §fslider§r on the right picks the height layer (16 blocks each)
- Layers are remembered: explored caves stay on the map

> Caves get on the map when you walk through them — the map draws what is around you.

# Biomes and search
- §bBiomes§r — colors the map by biome; hovering shows the biome at the bottom
- The §fsearch field§r shows up at the top in biome, ore or fluid view
- Example: type §e"Ocean"§r — everything else turns §7gray§r and oceans get §6outlined§r
- §eEsc§r in the field clears the search, §eEnter§r leaves the field

# Map view
- §bDay§r / §bNight§r — force a bright or dark map
- Both off — the map §ffollows the time of day§r
- §bGrid§r — the 16×16 chunk grid
- Mobs and players show as §fface icons§r; filters are in the settings §b(Entities)

# Ores and fluids
Needs the §fVisualProspecting§r mod (GTNH). The §bOres§r and §bFluids§r buttons are §fexclusive§r — only one is on.

- Hover a vein — a tooltip with its ores
- §eRight click a vein → Track§r — a marker with distance appears in the world
- §eRight click a vein → Depleted§r — marks the vein as mined out (drawn dim)
- The search at the top finds ores and fluids by name

! Veins show up only after prospecting (mined ores, samples, scanners).

# Claims (ServerUtilities)
Needs §fServerUtilities§r and §fNavigator§r on the server. Turn on the §bClaims§r button.

§fLeft drag a rectangle:§r
- §eCtrl§r — claim chunks
- §eShift§r — load chunks (chunkload), your own only
- §eCtrl+Shift§r — both

§fRight drag a rectangle:§r
- §eCtrl§r — unclaim
- §eShift§r — unload
- §eCtrl+Shift§r — §cremove everything§r (load and claim)

Colors: §9blue§r — your claims, §agreen§r — loaded, other teams in their team color.

! A selection is at most §f64×64§r chunks. Claim and load limits come from the server — see the counter at the bottom right.

# Settings
The §bSettings§r button on the map opens all options by category:

- §fMinimap§r — size, corner, zoom, coordinates and biome
- §fMap§r — caves, lighting, colors, scan speed, autosave
- §fEntities§r — players, hostile and passive mobs, icon limit, vertical range
- §fWaypoints§r — size, label width, max distance, in-world and minimap visibility

> Hover an option to see its description at the bottom.
